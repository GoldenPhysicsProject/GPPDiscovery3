# All-order supercritical massless-box moment closure

Codex/GPT discovery track, 2026-08-25.

This closes the parameter-space part of the supercritical `mu^(2r)` hierarchy for the massless one-loop box at every excess transverse power.

Use the standard simplex

\[
\Delta_3=\{a_i\ge0:\ a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4=1\}
\]

and the massless box Feynman polynomial

\[
F(a)=s\,a_1a_3+t\,a_2a_4
\]

in the convention already used by the `mu^8` gravity-box calculation.

For `m>=0`, expand

\[
F(a)^m=\sum_{j=0}^m {m\choose j}s^j t^{m-j}
 a_1^j a_3^j a_2^{m-j}a_4^{m-j}.
\]

The Dirichlet simplex moment is

\[
\int_{\Delta_3}a_1^j a_3^j a_2^{m-j}a_4^{m-j}\,da
=
\frac{(j!)^2((m-j)!)^2}{(2m+3)!}.
\]

Multiplying by the binomial coefficient gives the exact all-order closure

\[
\boxed{
\int_{\Delta_3}F(a)^m\,da
=
\frac{m!}{(2m+3)!}
\sum_{j=0}^m j!(m-j)!\,s^j t^{m-j}.
}
\]

Now specialize the supercritical hierarchy to `n=4`.  Then

\[
r=n-2+m=m+2,
\]

so the universal dimension-shift coefficient is

\[
(-1)^{m+1}\frac{(r-1)!}{m!}
=(-1)^{m+1}(m+1).
\]

Hence, in the same scalar-integral convention,

\[
\boxed{
I_4^{4-2\epsilon}[\mu^{2(m+2)}]
\longrightarrow
(-1)^{m+1}
\frac{(m+1)!}{(2m+3)!}
\sum_{j=0}^m j!(m-j)!\,s^j t^{m-j}.
}
\]

This is the complete one-loop massless-box rational residue hierarchy generated by excess transverse numerator power.

Low orders:

\[
m=0:\qquad I_4[\mu^4]\to -\frac16,
\]

\[
m=1:\qquad I_4[\mu^6]\to \frac{s+t}{60},
\]

\[
m=2:\qquad I_4[\mu^8]\to
-\frac{2s^2+st+2t^2}{840},
\]

recovering the previously checked four-graviton all-plus box value at `m=2`.

For `m=3`,

\[
I_4[\mu^{10}]\to
\frac{s^3}{2520}
+\frac{s^2t+st^2}{7560}
+\frac{t^3}{2520}.
\]

## Structural consequences

1. The residue is a homogeneous degree-`m` polynomial in `(s,t)`.
2. It is exactly symmetric under `s <-> t`.
3. The coefficient sequence is positive before the universal alternating dimension-shift sign.
4. The `mu^8` gravity box is not isolated: it is the `m=2` member of an explicit all-order box family.
5. This still does **not** by itself assemble a complete Yang-Mills or gravity amplitude. State sums, topology reduction, triangle/bubble/subtraction sectors, and generalized cuts remain separate sewing problems.

The companion script `audit_box_supercritical_moments.py` checks the Dirichlet/binomial formula symbolically for several orders and verifies the known `m=2` polynomial exactly.
